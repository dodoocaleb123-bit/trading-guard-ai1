## Concepts explicitly excluded from direct execution

The document discusses neural networks, commercial forecasting products, and very high predictive-accuracy claims. Those passages do not provide reproducible model parameters, training data, leakage controls, or inference procedures. v4 will not represent those claims as an internal neural network or as a promised accuracy improvement. Intermarket features may be added only through observable, timestamp-aligned provider data and will remain neutral when unavailable.

The document also contains absolute or marketing-style language about certainty, profit, and stops. v4 will not claim that a target must be hit, that a stop can never be reached, or that a given setup is guaranteed. These passages can inform risk and validation safeguards only.

## Proposed v4 scoring policy

V4 should score evidence by independent families rather than allowing every indicator to add a full vote. Structure/levels, price action, momentum, volatility/volume, timeframe, macro/event, and optional intermarket context should each have bounded contributions. Correlated indicators within one family should be capped. Conflicting observations should be retained in the trace and reduce confidence. Missing data should be neutral, not converted into an invented signal.

The decision path should remain deterministic: compute context, evaluate applicable source-linked components, reconcile conflicts, apply accepted lessons only when the pattern matches, derive structure-aware levels, apply event and geometry confidence adjustments, and emit a BUY or SELL paper outcome with complete provenance. The outcome remains `UNVALIDATED` until fresh forward evidence is sufficient for review.
